# BIR Form 2316 — Design

**Date:** 2026-09-06
**Status:** Approved for planning
**Follows:** the payslips phase; deferred from it because it needs year-to-date aggregation

## Context

Phases 1–4 built a payroll engine, fed it from real attendance, made it operable in the browser and
produced payslips. BIR Form 2316 — the annual Certificate of Compensation Payment and Tax Withheld —
was deliberately held back: a payslip renders one `PayrollRunEmployee` that already exists, while
2316 aggregates a whole year and needs facts PeopleCore does not hold.

Five tests were also held back from Phase 1 for the same reason. They assert Form 2316 Item 24
semantics — that **tax due** is the liability computed on Item 23, deliberately *not* the amount
withheld, and that their equality is precisely what qualifies an employee for substituted filing.
They depend on `Bir2316Dto` and land here.

**Goal:** produce Form 2316 PDFs for a calendar year, per employee or for everyone.

## Two deliberate departures from the PayZen source

### 1. Income is attributed by pay date, not period start

PayZen aggregates with `r.PeriodStart.Year == year`. A run covering 26 December to 10 January is then
attributed wholly to the earlier year.

BIR taxes compensation in the year it is **paid**, so this design uses `PayDate.Year`. December-into-
January periods are the ordinary case in a semi-monthly cycle, not an edge case, and getting this
wrong shifts income between tax years for a large fraction of the workforce.

### 2. Derived figures are recomputed server-side, never taken from the request

PayZen's generate endpoint accepts an entire `BIR2316Dto` in the request body and renders whatever it
is given — so a caller can post any withheld-tax figure they like and receive a tax certificate
stating it.

Here, generation recomputes every derived field from payroll data and accepts from the body **only**
the fields a human legitimately supplies: previous-employer block, de minimis, hazard pay, the
minimum-wage-earner flag and its rates, and any address or RDO override. A tax certificate is a legal
document; the money on it must come from the payroll records, not from the caller.

## Scope

### In

- `Employee.RdoCode`, `Employee.ZipCode`, `Company.RdoCode`, `Company.ZipCode` — one migration.
- `Bir2316Dto`, ported and renamed to PeopleCore's casing convention.
- A `Bir2316Service` that aggregates a year and builds the DTO.
- `Bir2316Document`, ported from PayZen with its layout preserved.
- Four endpoints: available years, preview, generate one, generate for a year.
- A Blazor page to preview, complete and download.
- The five Form 2316 tests deferred from Phase 1.

### Out

- Any UI for editing RDO codes and zips beyond adding them to the existing employee and company
  forms. They are ordinary fields on records those forms already edit.
- Electronic filing or BIR submission. This produces the PDF; filing is a separate concern.
- Alphalist or 1604-C. Related BIR forms, but different documents with different data.

## Aggregation

For an employee and a calendar year, over their entries in runs where `Status == Paid` and
`PayDate.Year == year`:

| Form item | Source |
|---|---|
| Item 39 — basic salary | sum of `RegularPay` |
| Item 50 — overtime | sum of `OvertimePay` |
| Item 36 — SSS/PhilHealth/Pag-IBIG | sum of `SSSEmployee + PhilHealthEmployee + PagIbigEmployee` |
| Item 34 — 13th month and benefits, non-taxable | `min(sum of ThirteenthMonth, 90,000)` |
| Item 48 — taxable 13th month | `max(0, sum of ThirteenthMonth - 90,000)` |
| Item 25A — tax withheld, present employer | sum of `WithholdingTax` |
| Period from / to | earliest `PeriodStart` and latest `PeriodEnd` among those runs |

The ₱90,000 figure is the statutory exemption cap for 13th-month pay and other benefits. It lives as
a named constant beside the tax tables in `PeopleCore.Domain.Payroll`, not inline — it changes by
legislation, and when it does, it must change in one place.

**Only `Paid` runs count.** A computed-but-unapproved run is not income yet, and `ComputeAsync` resets
a run to `Draft` on every recompute — so including anything earlier would let a tax certificate move
after it was issued. This is the same gate the self-service payslip path already applies.

**Item 24, tax due, is computed — not summed.** It is `BirWithholdingTax.ComputeAnnualTaxDue` applied
to Item 23, and is deliberately independent of Item 25's withheld total. The two agree only when
withholding was correct across the year, and that agreement is what makes an employee eligible for
substituted filing. Summing withheld tax into both would destroy the only signal the form exists to
carry. This is what the five deferred tests pin.

## What the form still asks a human for

The aggregation fills roughly twelve of the form's forty fields. The rest divide in two:

**Now stored, because they are stable per record:** employee RDO code and zip, employer RDO code and
zip. Re-typing an RDO code for two hundred employees every January is the failure mode that
guarantees a typo on somebody's tax certificate.

**Still supplied on the form, because they genuinely vary:** the previous-employer block, de minimis
benefits, hazard pay, and the minimum-wage-earner flag with its daily and monthly rates. These are
one-off figures an accountant provides per employee per year; storing them would model an annual
correction as if it were a standing fact.

## Flow

```
GET  years/{employeeId}        which years the employee has paid runs in
GET  preview/{employeeId}?year=  aggregated DTO, manual fields blank
      -> user completes the form in the browser
POST generate/{employeeId}?year=  body carries ONLY the manual fields
      -> service recomputes every derived figure and renders the PDF
POST generate-all?year=           the same, for every employee with paid runs
```

All four require `Admin`, `HRManager` or `PayrollService`. There is no self-service equivalent: an
employee's own 2316 is handed over by HR after review, not self-served from a draft.

## Error handling

- Employee not found → 404.
- No paid runs for that employee in that year → 404. There is nothing to certify.
- No company row → 400 naming the problem; the seeder creates one.
- A year in the future, or before the earliest paid run → 404 by the same rule.

## Testing

- The five Form 2316 tests deferred from Phase 1, ported unchanged in their assertions.
- Aggregation tests: sums across multiple paid runs; a Draft run excluded; a run whose `PayDate`
  falls in the next year excluded from this one and included in that one — the departure above,
  pinned by test.
- The 13th-month split at exactly ₱90,000, just below, and just above.
- A test proving generation ignores a derived figure supplied in the request body: post a
  `WithholdingTax` that contradicts the payroll records and assert the rendered figure comes from the
  records. This is the security departure, and it is the most important test in the phase.
- `Bir2316Document` renders, following the payslip document's test pattern.

The Blazor page is verified in a browser, as in earlier phases — this repository still has no
component tests.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A 2316 PDF can be generated for an employee and a year, from the browser.
2. Figures on it match the sum of that employee's paid runs for the year, by pay date.
3. A derived figure supplied in the request body is ignored, asserted by test.
4. A run paid in January is counted in January's year, not the prior December's, asserted by test.
5. Item 24 is computed from Item 23 rather than summed from withheld tax, asserted by the ported tests.
6. `dotnet build` is clean and the full suite is green.
7. The Phase 1 statutory files and their tests remain untouched.
